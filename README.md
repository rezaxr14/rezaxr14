## About me

Hi, I'm Reza. I'm an AI researcher and engineer working on vision-language models, RL-based fine-tuning and agentic AI. I do this research at **AIA CodeHive**, and I'm finishing an **M.S. in Mechatronics Engineering at Tehran University**.

My background spans mechanical engineering, robotics and control, machine learning and full-stack web development. That lets me work across the whole stack: training and evaluating models, building the backends and interfaces around them, and modeling the physical systems they may one day control. Two papers on agentic AI and harness engineering are in preparation.

I'm open to senior research-engineering and applied-AI roles in vision-language models, RL fine-tuning and agentic systems.

## What I work on

- **Vision-language and multimodal models:** tool-augmented reasoning, evaluation and calibration
- **RL-based fine-tuning:** SFT and GRPO pipelines with multi-objective rewards
- **Agentic AI and harness engineering**
- **Full-stack engineering:** Django / DRF, React, Next.js, TypeScript, Docker, CI
- **Robotics and control:** kinematics, RL control, embedded systems (STM32, PLC), Simulink / Simscape
